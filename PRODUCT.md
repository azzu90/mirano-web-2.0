# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: DACH-market decision-makers (CTOs, engineering leads, business owners) evaluating engineering partners for senior software teams, QA, or AI work — they're comparing Mirano against staffing agencies and larger consultancies, and want to know whether the people they'd actually work with are real, senior, and reachable. Secondary: smaller Croatian businesses looking for help with digitization and website relaunches. The site also speaks to a founder-led audience on /about specifically, since Mirano's story is the trust mechanism for the primary audience.

## Product Purpose

Mirano Solutions is an engineering partner for the DACH market and a product company at the same time: senior-led software teams from Zagreb (max. 40 experts, every profile personally known), delivered under EU contracts in the same time zone, alongside its own SaaS products (PLIMA, live since 2026, plus products in progress). The site exists to get a qualified visitor to talk directly to the founder — no account-manager layer, no CV database. Success looks like a founder call booked with someone who already believes the pool is real, senior, and low-risk to engage.

## Positioning

We build our own products and bring the same teams into your projects — proof of delivery quality is that we operate what we build, not a claim on a slide.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: "Talk to the founder" (primary); "See our references" / "Request team capacity" (secondary, for visitors not ready to book a call)
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: You talk to the founder, not an account manager.
- Belief ladder: (1) These are real senior engineers, not a CV database — max. 40 experts, personally known. (2) They've delivered comparable work — references and their own live products (PLIMA) are proof. (3) I'll talk directly to the founder, not an account layer. (4) Starting is low-risk — a free 30-minute call, kickoff in 5–10 business days.
- Proof on hand: partner logos (Headsquare, Serapion, Visoka Razina, Respect-ing, Mira Solis, If-Tech) in `public/logo/partners/`; anonymized case studies (insurance group Munich, accounting firm Croatia, premium-automotive UX engagement) via CaseTeaser/References; PLIMA as a live, in-production product (plima.cloud). No testimonial quotes — deliberately excluded per house style.

## Brand Personality

Founder-led, precise, understated. The voice is modest and technical rather than sales-polished: no self-praise, no testimonial quotes or credential lists used as bragging, no inflated growth claims. Confidence comes from specificity (real numbers, real dates, a named founder, a capped team size) rather than superlatives. Direct communication is both a value and a literal product feature — visitors reach the founder, not a layer of account management.

## Anti-references

Generic agency/SaaS template look: stock-photo consultancy sites, gradient-hero "AI startup" templates, bloated enterprise-agency sites with fake testimonials, and inflated team-size or growth claims. Mirano's smallness (max. 40 experts) and specificity are the opposite of that register — the design should never read as trying to look bigger or more generic-corporate than it is.

## Design Principles

- Specificity over superlatives — real dates, real numbers, a named founder, capped pool size; never vague trust badges or generic claims.
- Show, don't tell — proof comes from partner logos, anonymized case studies, and a live product (PLIMA), not testimonial quotes or self-description.
- Practice what you preach — Mirano operates its own SaaS products, and the site should reflect the same delivery discipline it sells (precise, no shortcuts, senior craft).
- Direct line, no layers — every path should feel like it leads to the founder, not a sales funnel; understated over persuasive.
- Quality over growth — the design should never visually imply scale or size the company doesn't have.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA baseline: standard contrast and keyboard-navigation requirements. Reduced-motion support required (partially present in `tokens.css` already — `prefers-reduced-motion` disables transitions/animations globally). Trilingual site (DE/EN/HR via `src/i18n`) — new UI copy and components must support all three locales, not just German.
